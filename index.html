About AgriShare Portal

Project Scope

Transforming Agricultural Machinery Access

Modern agriculture demands heavy farm mechanization to achieve optimal crop yields, yet over 82% of farmers in India operate on small and marginal landholdings where purchasing tractors (₹7–12 Lakhs) or combine harvesters (₹25–35 Lakhs) is financially prohibitive. AgriShare provides an on-demand, peer-to-peer equipment sharing infrastructure that enables machinery owners to monetize idle machinery during off-season periods while giving local farmers access to top-tier equipment at transparent hourly rental rates.

KNOWLEDGE BASE & GUIDELINES

Help Center & Farmer FAQs

How do I rent equipment?

Complete the Registration form above, browse the catalog, select your required date and hours, and click Rent Machinery to send a booking request to the verified owner.

Is security deposit required?

No mandatory advance deposit is required for verified local farmers. All registered machinery carries insurance coverage against accidental field breakdowns.

Are trained drivers included?

Yes, heavy machinery such as Combine Harvesters and Laser Levelers include certified operators at standard hourly wage increments.
