About Us

Welcome to AgriShare

AgriShare is a Farmer Equipment Sharing and Rental Portal designed to connect equipment owners with farmers. The platform enables farmers to rent agricultural machinery instead of purchasing expensive equipment, helping reduce operational costs and improve farming efficiency.

Vision

To make agricultural machinery accessible to every farmer through digital technology.

Mission

To promote affordable farming by enabling secure equipment sharing and efficient booking management.

Platform Features

  • Equipment Listing
  • Equipment Search
  • Online Booking
  • Rental Tracking
  • Notifications
  • Reports
  • Admin Dashboard
  • Secure Login
